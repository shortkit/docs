{/*
  How to write an entry (read before adding one)

  This page is curated, not a release log. Add an entry when a release changes something a
  customer would act on or notice: a new capability, a behaviour change, a deprecation, a
  fix for a bug customers reported, or a portal change worth showing. Routine SDK patch
  releases do not get an entry; fold them into the next notable one.

  - One <Update> per announcement, newest first. `label` is the date ("October 4, 2026") and
    becomes the anchor (#october-4-2026). A second entry on the same day gets
    #october-4-2026-2 on the page, so add it below the first and never reorder them. The
    RSS feed links both to #october-4-2026, so the first one should be the one announced.
  - `description` is the short name shown under the date: "SDK 0.2.91", "Portal", "API".
  - `tags` come from this list only, so the filters stay short:
    iOS, Android, React Native, Flutter, API, Portal.
  - `rss` with a `title` is required: without it, Mintlify makes one feed item per `##`
    heading. Write the title as a headline.
  - `rss.description` decides whether the portal announces the entry. It is never shown
    on this page; it is the one-sentence blurb on the portal's "What's new" card. Leave it
    out and the entry is quiet: here and in RSS for anyone looking, never in the portal.
  - An image decides how loudly the portal announces the entry. With one (a Markdown image,
    `![alt](/images/changelog/...)`, at the top), the entry is a launch: it pops up as a
    card over the corner of the portal, picture first. Without one, it waits quietly in
    the portal's sidebar. Use an image only for the few releases worth interrupting for.
  - SDK entries say what a customer gets, never how the SDK does it: name the capability
    ("protected playback is more reliable"), not players, pools, caches or tokens.
  - Link to the guide for anything new. Never mention customers, internal ticket ids or
    unreleased work.
*/}

  ![The redesigned portal dashboard](/images/changelog/portal-redesign.png)

  ## A redesigned admin portal

  We rebuilt the admin portal from the ground up. Every page is simpler to read, the things
  you check most are easier to find, and it now works on a phone.

  - **Usage, rethought.** **Settings → Usage** shows what you've used this period against what
    your plan includes, for storage, encoding and delivery, with a projection for the rest of
    the period and a day-by-day view of each.
  - **A calmer content drawer.** Opening a video shows what matters first: its status,
    preview and key details. Everything else is a click away instead of on one long panel.
  - **Analytics overhaul.** New filters, period-over-period comparisons on every headline
    number, and a definition behind each metric: hover the ⓘ to see exactly what's counted.
    More is on the way.
  - **Themes.** Choose Light, Dark or System from the account menu at the foot of the
    sidebar, or from **Settings → Account → Appearance**.
  - **Audio tracks.** A video's details list its audio tracks, and the content library can
    show which videos carry more than one.
  - **A collapsible sidebar.** Fold the sidebar down to icons for more room, and the portal
    remembers your choice. On a phone, the sidebar opens from the menu in the top bar.
</Update>

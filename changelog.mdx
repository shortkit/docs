{/*
  How to write an entry (read before adding one)

  This page is curated, not a release log. Add an entry when a release changes something a
  customer would act on or notice: a new capability, a behaviour change, a deprecation, a
  fix for a bug customers reported, or a portal change worth showing. Routine SDK patch
  releases do not get an entry; fold them into the next notable one.

  - One <Update> per announcement, newest first. `label` is the date ("October 4, 2026") and
    must be unique on the page, because it becomes the anchor (#october-4-2026). Two
    announcements on one day share an entry, with an `##` heading for each.
  - `description` is the short name shown under the date: "SDK 0.2.91", "Portal", "API".
  - `tags` come from this list only, so the filters stay short:
    iOS, Android, React Native, Flutter, API, Portal.
  - `rss` is required. Its title and description are what RSS readers get and what the
    portal's "What's new" card shows, so write them as a headline and one sentence.
    Without it, Mintlify makes one feed item per `##` heading.
  - Link to the guide for anything new. Never mention customers, internal ticket ids or
    unreleased work.
*/}

>
  ## A redesigned portal

  Every page of the portal has been redrawn: the dashboard, content library, tags, settings
  and organization picker. Navigation is unchanged, and on a phone the sidebar now opens
  from a menu in the top bar.

  - **Themes.** Choose Light, Dark, or System from the account menu at the foot of the
    sidebar, or from **Settings → Account → Appearance**.
  - **Audio tracks.** A video's details now list its audio tracks, and the content library
    can show which videos carry more than one.

  ## SDK 0.2.91

  Released for iOS, Android and React Native.

  - **Protected playback.** Hardening for organizations whose whole library is protected.
    See [Protected content](/guides/protected-content).
  - **Mute per surface.** Muting a feed, player or widget no longer mutes the others.
  - **Android.** Tile previews and cached feed stills recover correctly after a token
    refresh, and stills decode at a bounded size to save memory.
</Update>
